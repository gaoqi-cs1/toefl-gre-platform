[TRANSCRIPT]
Student: Hi, I booked a study room for this afternoon, but the confirmation email never arrived.
Librarian: Let me check. Rooms are held for fifteen minutes, so if you come a little late, please call the front desk.
Student: Understood. Could you resend the confirmation to my university address?
Librarian: Sure. It should reach you within a few minutes.
[/TRANSCRIPT]


[TRANSLATION]
学生：你好，我订了今天下午的自习室，但确认邮件一直没收到。
图书管理员：我查一下。房间只保留十五分钟，如果你来晚一点，请给前台打个电话。
学生：明白了。能不能把确认邮件重发到我的学校邮箱？
图书管理员：可以，几分钟内应该就到你那里了。
[/TRANSLATION]

[Q]
type: PURPOSE
stem: Why does the student speak to the librarian?
A) To reserve a room for next week
B) To complain about a late fee
C) To ask about a missing confirmation email
D) To return an overdue book
answer: C
analysis: 学生开头就说 booked a study room 但 confirmation email never arrived，说明来意是确认邮件没到，故选 C。A 是已订而非再订；B 未提费用；D 未提还书。
[/Q]
